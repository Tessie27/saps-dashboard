SAPS figures are official recorded-crime statistics (the "17 community-reported serious crimes" series unless noted) at station, provincial and national level, FY2015-2016 to FY2024-2025.
Per-100,000 rates use Stats SA mid-year provincial population estimates (approximate) and are for relative comparison only. Map station markers are shown only for major metro stations with known coordinates; all other stations appear in the leaderboard table below.
Gender breakdown is from a different source: Statistics South Africa, P0341 — Victims of Crime Survey / Governance, Public Safety and Justice Survey (GPSJS) 2025/26, published 1 September 2026, a household interview survey that also captures crime never reported to police. Household-crime figures are broken down by the sex of the household head; individual-crime figures by the sex of the person affected. It is not directly comparable to the SAPS recorded-crime totals above.
